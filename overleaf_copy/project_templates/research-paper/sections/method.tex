\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Equations are numbered automatically:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \ell\bigl(f_\theta(x_i), y_i\bigr).
  \label{eq:loss}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}
If Equation~\eqref{eq:loss} is convex in $\theta$, every local minimum is global.
\end{theorem}
